# Vision And Product Thesis

## Product vision

Build the best horse-transporter operating system for small and mid-sized transport businesses that currently lose time, confidence, and enquiries to manual quoting and fragmented lead handling.

Near term, that means a private quoting and operations portal for one transporter team.

Long term, that means a transporter-facing SaaS and marketplace that helps transport businesses:

- win more enquiries
- quote faster
- understand route economics more clearly
- manage work without spreadsheets
- eventually match against nearby journeys and spare capacity

## The operator problem

The current spreadsheet logic is not the hard part. The hard part is the time, friction, and uncertainty around turning an inbound request into a quote quickly enough to win work without damaging pricing discipline.

Today the business still has to bridge too much manually:

- enquiry arrives from word of mouth, social media, HayNet, or direct contact
- operator gathers partial details
- operator works out route legs manually
- operator enters or estimates mileage
- operator prices the quote
- operator explains or edits the total
- operator follows up later through a loosely structured pipeline

That is not yet a product-grade flow.

## Why the current app is still pre-MVP

The current app is pre-MVP because the core promise is still missing:

- route mileage is still manual
- the operator must still think like the spreadsheet in order to finish a quote
- the workflow is still system-shaped instead of operator-shaped
- the app helps with pricing integrity, but not yet with quoting speed

If the operator cannot enter postcodes and get a calculated three-leg quote path quickly, the app is not yet replacing the spreadsheet in the way that matters most.

## North-star outcome

The north-star outcome for the near-term product is:

An operator receives an inbound transport enquiry, enters the essential journey details, gets an automatically resolved three-leg route and transparent quote total, applies a controlled manual override only if needed, and issues the quote in a couple of minutes.

That is the first outcome the product must own before broader workflow polish or marketplace expansion matters.

## Primary customer

The primary customer is the horse transport business.

More specifically:

- the business owner who wants better efficiency, faster quoting, and more confidence in pricing
- the operator or staff member who has to respond to enquiries quickly
- later, additional transporter businesses who would pay for this system as software

Horse owners and people requesting transport matter, but they are not the paying customer in the current product thesis.

## Near-term and long-term product split

## Near-term product

Private operator portal for a single transporter business.

Purpose:

- reduce quote turnaround time
- make route-based pricing reliable
- keep auditability and revision control
- support real daily staff use

Core value:

- internal efficiency
- confidence in quote logic
- less spreadsheet dependency

## Long-term product

Transporter-facing SaaS and marketplace.

Purpose:

- help transport businesses attract and manage leads
- provide better quoting and operational tooling than directory-only competitors
- create future network effects through route awareness, transport availability, and lead matching

Core value:

- acquisition
- retention
- operational lock-in through better workflow tools

## Product thesis

The product thesis is:

Horse transport businesses will pay for software that combines lead access with a much better quoting and operations workflow than they can get from listing directories or spreadsheets alone.

Why this is credible:

- HayNet proves there is value in horse-transport discovery and quote-request demand
- the current business context proves transport operators already work from mixed inbound channels
- the existing app proves pricing logic can be made deterministic and inspectable
- field-service software patterns show that speed, route context, and workflow integration are the real usability moat

## Commercial model, hypothesis level

This is not final pricing policy. It is a working product hypothesis for planning.

## Tier 1

Listing and enquiry access.

Likely value proposition:

- advertise as a transporter
- receive relevant quote enquiries
- maintain a visible business profile
- participate in the network without adopting the full operational system

## Tier 2

Listing plus quoting and operations system.

Likely value proposition:

- everything in Tier 1
- automatic route-based quoting
- revision-backed pricing records
- internal quote and pipeline workflow
- future route matching and spare-capacity features
- stronger retention through everyday operational dependency

## Why transporter acquisition and retention come first

The long-term platform should optimise for transporter acquisition and retention first because transporters are the recurring revenue base.

That means:

- better operator tools are not side features, they are the monetisation engine
- lead distribution only works if transporters trust and stay on the platform
- route-aware matching becomes stronger when the transporter-side data model is already in place

## What this means for the next phase

The next phase should not chase breadth.

It should rescue the MVP by making the private portal genuinely useful for the main quoting job first:

- automatic route mileage
- fast quote generation
- transparent pricing
- controlled exceptions
- issueable quote outcome

Everything else, including broader marketplace concepts, should be documented now but built later.
