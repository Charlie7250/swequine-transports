# Release 1 Open Decisions

## Purpose

These are the decisions that still require stakeholder sign-off before or during Release 1 build work. The recommended defaults make the planning set actionable, but they are not hidden policy. The named owner must either approve the default or record a different choice before the specified delivery slice begins.

Owners use roles because named individuals are not yet recorded in the strategy material.

| ID | Decision | Why it matters | Options | Recommended default | Owner | Due before |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | Paid routing provider and commercial plan | Automatic routing is MVP-critical. Coverage, cost, quota, latency, and terms affect whether the normal flow is viable. | Select a provider after a short evaluation; delay Release 1; use a temporary provider only for staging. | Select one paid provider with UK postcode coverage, suitable quota, support route distance and diagnostics, and staging credentials. No permanent manual-mile alternative. | Business owner with technical owner | Slice 2 |
| D2 | Routing profile and material provider options | The route profile determines the mileage the pricing engine receives and therefore operator trust. | Standard driving profile; provider-specific commercial-vehicle profile; another documented profile. | Use the provider's standard driving profile unless the business identifies a vehicle restriction that materially changes normal routes. Store profile metadata on every resolution. | Business owner | Slice 1 |
| D3 | Minimum information for an issued quote | It determines when an enquiry can become quote-ready and prevents unrecorded assumptions. | Require requested date; allow date-to-be-arranged; allow provisional issue with less contact or constraint data. | Require name, contact method, source, postcodes, horse count, date or explicit date-to-be-arranged, constraints acknowledged, active pricing context, complete route basis, and review confirmation. | Business owner | Slice 1 |
| D4 | Provisional-quote policy and customer-facing wording | The workflow must not imply that an incomplete or date-dependent price is a firm commitment. | No provisional quotes in Release 1; issue labelled provisional quotes; treat date-to-be-arranged as a normal firm quote. | Support `date to be arranged` as recorded context, but do not add a separate provisional issue state until the business supplies approved wording and policy. | Business owner | Slice 5 |
| D5 | Route warning and operator-review thresholds | These settings decide which credible route results need attention without turning warnings into noise. | No application thresholds beyond provider flags; configure mileage anomaly threshold; configure repeat-route comparison. | Start with provider ambiguity and explicit provider advisories only. Add numeric anomaly thresholds only after representative route data exists. | Business owner with technical owner | Slice 2 |
| D6 | Manual-mile fallback authority and reason categories | Manual mileage is needed for exceptions but must not become an untracked routine. | Any staff member may use it; named operators only; require supervisor approval; structured reasons only; structured reasons plus free text. | Permit trained staff to use it with structured reason plus free text where needed, operator identity, timestamp, and full changed-leg audit. Review rate during pilot. | Business owner | Slice 4 |
| D7 | Final-total override authority and reason categories | The final total is customer-facing and authoritative for reporting, so commercial discretion needs a clear owner. | Any staff member; named approvers; value-based approval thresholds; no final-total override. | Allow the existing final-total override only to operators authorised by the business owner, always with a reason and retained engine total. Do not add unapproved value thresholds in Release 1. | Business owner | Slice 4 |
| D8 | Route and provider-response retention policy | Audit needs reproducible route evidence, but retention must be proportionate and avoid unnecessary personal data. | Store full sanitised response; store normalised fields plus fingerprint; retain for quote lifetime; retain for a fixed period. | Retain the structured contract result and sanitised provider metadata with the quote revision. Decide separately whether raw provider payloads are needed before implementation. | Technical owner | Slice 1 |
| D9 | Issued quote output and issue channel | Release 1 needs an output the business can actually rely on, but automated email is out of scope. | Printable/downloadable output; copyable quote summary; manual send outside the portal; defer issue until an output exists. | Provide an approved staff-facing issueable quote output that can be copied or downloaded for manual sending. Do not implement email sending or ingestion. | Business owner | Slice 5 |
| D10 | Quote-turnaround and routing-success thresholds | Release approval needs measurable evidence rather than a subjective impression. | Approve the proposed targets; set different targets after provider testing; use observation only. | Use the proposed staged targets: median complete-enquiry-to-issued under 3 minutes and at least 95 percent automatic three-leg success across a sample of at least 20 valid route attempts. | Business owner with technical owner | Slice 3 |
| D11 | Pilot scope and production fallback | Release 1 can be privately piloted, but the business must decide who uses it and what happens during a sustained routing outage. | Staging only; one-operator pilot; limited named-staff pilot; immediately make it default. | Run a controlled named-staff private-production pilot only after the Release 1 gate. Keep the spreadsheet available as a documented contingency until later release gates are met. | Business owner with technical owner | Slice 5 |
| D12 | Release and operational ownership | Staging and private production require accountable approval, deployment, rollback, credentials, and backup ownership. | Name one technical owner and one business owner; delegate by environment; defer production. | Name one business owner for workflow acceptance and one technical owner for deployment, routing credentials, backups, rollback, and monitoring before pilot promotion. | Business owner | Slice 5 |

## Decision rule

If a decision due before a slice has no approved answer, that slice must not start. This prevents provider choice, release policy, or operator authority being accidentally encoded as implementation detail.
